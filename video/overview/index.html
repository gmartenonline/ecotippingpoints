Video (3 minutes) Ecotipping Points: Success Stories for Sustainability

This introductory video explains how small community actions can trigger lasting environmental and social improvements. Examples from Apo Island in the Philippines and New York City show how protecting a fishery and creating community gardens helped restore ecosystems and revitalize neighborhoods. The video introduces the EcoTipping Points Project and its collection of success stories, offering practical inspiration for communities facing their own challenges.
